Frequently asked questions

What should I tell the restoration team before they arrive?

Describe where you noticed water, whether it is still entering, and which rooms are affected. Mention any known electrical hazards, sewage involvement, or areas that are difficult to access.

Should I move belongings out of an affected room?

Move items only when the area is safe to enter and handling them will not spread contamination. Ask for guidance before carrying damp or visibly mold-affected belongings through clean rooms.

Does a musty smell identify the source of a problem?

An odor can be a reason to investigate, but it does not reveal the location or extent of moisture damage by itself. Share where and when you notice the smell so the assessment can consider that information.
