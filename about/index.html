Prepare for Your First Conversation

A few details can make an initial inquiry easier. Have the property location ready, explain the damage or concern in everyday terms, and mention when you first noticed it. If another contractor has already addressed a leak or completed repairs, share that information too.

Before traveling to the listed address, call to discuss your needs and ask how to arrange contact with the team. This gives you an opportunity to clarify whether your inquiry concerns a property assessment, cleanup, or another restoration service. You can also ask what information would be helpful for the next conversation.
