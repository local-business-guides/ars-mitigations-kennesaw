Planning Mold and Water Restoration in Kennesaw

Mold concerns and water damage often belong in the same conversation. Removing visible growth does not resolve an ongoing plumbing leak, roof problem, or other source of dampness. A restoration discussion should include the current condition of the space and any steps already taken to stop water from entering.

Useful questions to ask include:

  • Which parts of the property need evaluation?
  • What work is proposed, and why?
  • How will cleanup and any needed repairs be coordinated?
  • What information should occupants have before work begins?

Keep photographs and a brief timeline if you can collect them without entering damaged areas. Tell the team about previous repairs or recurring dampness so those details can be considered when discussing the next steps.
